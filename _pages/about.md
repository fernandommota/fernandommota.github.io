I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the Faculty of Computing ([FACOM](https://www.facom.ufms.br/)) of the Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS), Brazil, advised by Prof. Edson Takashi Matsubara and co-advised by Prof. Jonathan de Andrade Silva.

**Research.** My doctoral research is on **text anonymization under the inference capabilities of large language models**. Removing names and other explicit identifiers from a document is no longer enough: a language model can often work out who a person is, or sensitive facts about them, from the context that remains. I study how to measure this re-identification risk and how to anonymize documents so that they stay useful for the tasks they are shared for. My application domain is legal documents in Brazilian Portuguese.

**Background.** Before the PhD, I spent more than a decade building data platforms in industry: data warehouses, ETL/ELT pipelines, data lakehouses and analytics for agribusiness, construction and the public sector. I still work on tools for that world, such as [PBI Grid](https://github.com/OESNPG/pbi-grid), a dashboard-as-code compiler for Power BI, and Rampart, a framework for reproducible benchmarks of data-processing engines.

I hold a BSc in Information Systems (2011) and a professional MSc in Applied Computing (2016), both from UFMS. My master's work combined OLAP and data mining to support decision making in beef cattle production. As an undergraduate, I worked on publishing statistical data as Linked Data with researchers from PUC-Rio and the University of Leipzig.

**Interests:** privacy in natural language processing, large language models, information extraction, knowledge graphs and data engineering.
